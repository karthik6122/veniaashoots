Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Veniaa Shoots ("we", "us") films reels and event coverage in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. This page explains what information this website collects, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us:

  • Booking form: your name, mobile number, and optionally your email, event date, city, package and shoot details.
  • WhatsApp, phone and email: anything you share with us when you contact us directly.

The booking form does not save your details on this website. When you press send, your details are placed into a WhatsApp message that you send to us yourself.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, check availability and send you a quote.
  • To plan, shoot and deliver your reels.
  • To contact you about your booking.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone for advertising.

Services we rely on

  • WhatsApp (Meta) and Gmail (Google), when you contact us through them. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Website hosting, which may keep standard server logs such as your IP address and browser type, used for security and keeping the site running.
  • Google Fonts, which loads the fonts on this site. Google receives your IP address when the fonts load.
  • Instagram, only if you click a link to our Instagram page.

Cookies and similar storage

This website does not use advertising, tracking or analytics cookies. It stores only a small setting in your browser:

  • vs-consent: remembers your choice on the cookie notice so it doesn't keep appearing.
  • vs-theme: remembers whether you picked light or dark mode.

You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of the website, or clear it by deleting this site's data in your browser settings. If we ever add analytics, we will update this page and only turn it on if you choose "Accept all".

Photos and videos from your shoot

Footage from your shoot is backed up to secure cloud storage. We only post your reels on our own Instagram or website as samples of our work if you agree. If you'd like a reel taken down from our pages, just tell us.

How long we keep information

We keep enquiry and booking details only as long as needed to handle your booking and any follow-up, and delete them when they are no longer needed or when you ask.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the details we hold about you at any time using the contact details below.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
